Data Privacy Notice & User Consent

In compliance with Republic Act No. 10173 (Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012), Campus Chicken SmartStall hereby notifies you that by registering an account and using this pre-ordering platform, you consent to the collection, processing, and storage of the following personal data:

📋 Personal Information:

Full Name, Email Address, and Account Credentials (encrypted passwords).

🧾 Transaction Data:

List of selected food items, Timestamp of orders, Payment Method (GCash transaction status or Cash), and Queue Completion Records.

🎯 Purpose of Data Processing:

Your personal data is collected for account authentication and meal queue ticket processing, live preparation progress updates, and demand prediction analytics reporting of aggregated, anonymized food items statistics for inventory management purposes. Your financial data and sensitive information such as passwords are not processed or stored by the system.

🔒 Data Protection & Rights:

All encrypted information is processed using industry-standard hashing algorithms. You may request the erasure of your personal data or account information at any time by contacting the system administrators.
